# Privacy Policy

This policy covers all of Aria's apps and tools. "We" means Aria's developer.

Aria is built to keep your voice and words on your own devices. It has no
accounts, no ads, and no analytics. We never receive your recordings,
transcripts, or settings.

The one exception is crash reports. If you opt in through your device
settings, your platform provider, such as Apple, may share crash reports and
usage statistics with us.

## What stays on your device

Aria processes speech on your devices and keeps your recordings, transcripts,
voice profiles, and settings there. You can delete them at any time.

## What leaves your device

Data leaves your device in these cases:

- **Downloading models and updates.** As with any download, the sites that
  host them see basic details such as your IP address.
- **Using an AI service you've set up.** Some features send what you ask to
  that service. Its own privacy policy applies.
- **Using Aria across your devices.** When you connect your devices, Aria
  sends data between them over encrypted connections.
